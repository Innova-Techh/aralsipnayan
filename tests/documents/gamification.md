# AralSipnayan Gamification System

## 🎯 Points System

### Base Points per Question (base points regardless of correct or incorrect)
- **Beginner Question**: 3 points per any attempt
- **Intermediate Question**: 6 points per any attempt
- **Advanced Question**: 8 points per any attempt

### Time Response Bonus

- **Fast Response + correct**: +15 bonus points
- **Medium Fast Response + correct**: +13 bonus points
- **Slow Response + correct**: +10 bonus points
- **Any Response + Incorrect**: +5 bonus points

### Daily Streak Bonus
- **Daily Login Streak**: +5 points per consecutive day

---

## 📊 Level System

- **Total Levels**: 100
- **Points per Level**: 60 points
- **Maximum Points**: 6,000 points

### Level Progression
Each level requires exactly 60 points to advance, creating consistent and predictable progression for students.

---

## 🏆 Rank System with Insignia

Ranks are awarded every 10 levels, providing major milestone achievements:

- **Level range 1-10 - 🌱 Math Explorer** (540 points): Starting the journey
- **Level range 20-29 - 🚀 Math Adventurer** (1,140 points): Gaining confidence and exploring new challenges  
- **Level range 30-30 - 🔍 Math Seeker** (1,740 points): Developing problem-solving skills and curiosity
- **Level range 40-49 - 🧠 Math Strategist** (2,340 points): Learning to think critically and apply strategies
- **Level range 50-59 - 💡 Math Innovator** (2,940 points): Solving problems creatively and independently
- **Level range 60-69 - ⭐ Math Prodigy** (3,540 points): Recognized for impressive math mastery and speed
- **Level range 70-79 - 🎭 Math Virtuoso** (4,140 points): Demonstrating exceptional mathematical skills
- **Level range 80-89 - 🧙 Math Sage** (4,740 points): Reaching a higher understanding of concepts and patterns
- **Level range 90-99- 🏅 Math Champion** (5,340 points): Competing at an elite level and mastering challenges
- **Level range 100 - 👑 Math Grandmaster** (5,940 points): The ultimate achievement

## 🏆 Competitive Elements

### Leaderboard Trophies

#### Class Leaderboard
- **Participants**: Top 10 students
- **Criteria**: Total points earned each week
- **Reset**: Every Monday at 12:00 AM

#### School Leaderboard
- **Participants**: Top 20 students
- **Criteria**: Total points earned each month
- **Reset**: First day of each month at 12:00 AM

---

## 🔥 Streak System

### Daily Assessment Streak
- **Requirement**: Complete at least 1 assessment per day
- **Reward**: Maintains streak counter
- **Bonus**: +5 points per login day with active streak

### Streak Benefits
- Encourages daily engagement
- Builds consistent learning habits
- Provides additional points for dedicated students

---

## 📈 Progression Summary

### Point Earning Potential
- **Minimum per question**: 8 points (3 base + 5 slow bonus)
- **Maximum per question**: 25 points (10 base + 15 fast bonus)
- **Daily streak bonus**: +5 points per day

### Time to Rank Advancement
- **Conservative estimate**: 24-75 questions per rank (600 points)
- **Active student estimate**: 14-40 questions per rank
- **High performer estimate**: 8-22 questions per rank

This gamification system is designed to motivate elementary students through clear progression, meaningful rewards, and competitive elements while maintaining educational focus.
